\section{Profile}
Research engineer specialising in LLM post-training optimisation, distributed inference performance, and low-precision methods for frontier models. I benchmark LLMs at scale on 2$\times$ NVIDIA H100 NVL and H100 SXM5 hardware — profiling TTFT/TPOT bottlenecks across vLLM, SGLang, and TensorRT-LLM — and have fine-tuned Mistral 7B, Phi-4, and Qwen3 models using PEFT/LoRA pipelines with rigorous evaluation frameworks. My CUDA KV cache kernel profiling on RTX 4090 with NVIDIA Nsight Systems identified memory bandwidth bottlenecks and delivered 3.8$\times$ throughput improvement. Passionate about applying LLM performance engineering to high-stakes scientific domains. Open to London in-person. MSc AI with Distinction, University of Strathclyde 2023.
