I am applying for the Staff Software Engineer, AI Reliability Engineering role at Anthropic. Three achievements directly relevant to this role:

\begin{itemize}

Result: 100\% SLO compliance at every concurrency level. RadixAttention prefix caching delivered 1,443$\times$ TTFT speedup (39 ms vs 55,565 ms) at 131K-token context. Live results at \href{https://inference-web-theta.vercel.app/benchmarks/llm}{inference-web-theta.vercel.app} — this is the kind of rigour I apply to SLO work.

  \item \textbf{Full observability stack for LLM serving:} I built \textit{llm-inference-platform} — production LLM serving with Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards (GPU utilisation, queue depth, per-request latency), Loki log aggregation, AlertManager paging, and Velero backup/restore. I also built \textit{vllm-k8s-stack} with KV-cache-aware routing and HPA autoscaling, and configured KEDA for GPU-aware scale-to-zero. Every component is wired for observability-first operations.

Diagnosing unfamiliar systems under production pressure is a working mode, not an edge case for me.
\end{itemize}
