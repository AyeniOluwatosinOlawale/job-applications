\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am applying for the Staff ML Performance Engineer (Inference Optimisation) role at Wayve. I bring production-grade experience in GPU inference optimisation — CUDA profiling, KV cache tuning, serving framework benchmarking on H100 hardware — and I am genuinely excited about the inference challenges involved in deploying large end-to-end neural driving models at scale.

Three specific achievements relevant to what Wayve is building:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H100 inference benchmarks — production scale:} I benchmarked vLLM v0.30 against SGLang v0.5.20 on 2$\times$ H100 NVL 94 GB GPUs (NVLink 4.0, CUDA 13.4) running Qwen3-8B BF16 at tensor parallelism 2. I measured TTFT, TPOT, and throughput across concurrency 1--128, achieving 5,965 tok/s peak and identifying RadixAttention prefix caching as delivering a 1,443$\times$ TTFT speedup (39 ms vs 55,565 ms) at 131K-token context — 100\% SLO compliance throughout. Results: \href{https://inference-web-theta.vercel.app/benchmarks/llm}{inference-web-theta.vercel.app/benchmarks/llm}.

  \item \textbf{CUDA kernel profiling — KV cache bottleneck:} Using NVIDIA Nsight Systems and Compute, I profiled KV cache CUDA operations on RTX 4090 hardware, identified the memory bandwidth bottleneck, and retuned the kernel access pattern to deliver 3.8$\times$ throughput improvement without a hardware change. This required reading warp occupancy reports and memory transaction traces directly — not just running benchmarking scripts.

  \item \textbf{Production serving stack on Kubernetes:} I built \textit{vllm-k8s-stack} — production LLM serving with vLLM, KV-cache-aware request routing, HPA autoscaling on GPU node pools, and Prometheus/Grafana observability. This is the infrastructure pattern I would bring to Wayve's inference deployment challenges.
\end{itemize}

Wayve's approach of training one end-to-end neural model rather than modular sensor pipelines is what first caught my attention. The inference throughput and latency requirements for real-time autonomous driving decisions are some of the most demanding in the industry — and they sit exactly at the boundary of model architecture and serving infrastructure that I have been building toward.

I would welcome a conversation about how my benchmarking and optimisation work translates to Wayve's production inference stack. I am open to relocating to London.
