\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am writing to apply for the Member of Technical Staff: Inference Research role at Doubleword. Your focus on multi-LoRA serving, GPU architecture, and speculative decoding is exactly the class of problem I have been building toward — and I have the benchmarks to prove it.

Three specific achievements relevant to this role:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H100 NVL inference research — RadixAttention and prefix caching:} I benchmarked vLLM v0.30 vs SGLang v0.5.20 on 2$\times$ NVIDIA H100 NVL 94 GB (NVLink 4.0, CUDA 13.4), Qwen3-8B BF16, TP=2. The critical finding was the impact of RadixAttention prefix caching in memory-constrained long-context settings: 1,443$\times$ TTFT speedup (39 ms vs 55,565 ms) at 131K-token context, with 100\% SLO compliance at concurrency 128 and peak throughput of 5,965 tok/s. Results are live at \href{https://inference-web-theta.vercel.app/benchmarks/llm}{inference-web-theta.vercel.app/benchmarks/llm}.

  \item \textbf{CUDA kernel profiling and speculative decoding experiments:} Using NVIDIA Nsight Systems and Compute, I profiled KV cache CUDA ops on RTX 4090 hardware — identified memory bandwidth as the bottleneck and retuned kernel access patterns for 3.8$\times$ throughput improvement. I also ran speculative decoding experiments across vLLM and SGLang to characterise speedup vs draft model overhead tradeoffs, documented in \textit{open-model-engine} (\href{https://github.com/AyeniOluwatosinOlawale/open-model-engine}{github.com/AyeniOluwatosinOlawale/open-model-engine}).

  \item \textbf{Multi-modal LLM inference — vLLM-Omni:} I built \textit{lab-vllm-omni}, a phased inference lab covering all 5 output modalities of vLLM-Omni (text, audio, image, video, structured output) — characterising throughput, memory pressure, and scheduling behaviour across modalities. This is directly relevant to serving diverse inference workloads efficiently as Doubleword scales.
\end{itemize}

Doubleword's approach — small team, deep technical work, research-to-production focus — is exactly what I want. I am based in Glasgow and happy to relocate to London for the right role; Farringdon 2--3 days/week is workable.
